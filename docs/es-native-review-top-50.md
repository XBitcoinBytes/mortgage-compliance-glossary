Prepared 25 November 2026 (America/Detroit) for a future native-speaker review of the Mortgage Compliance Glossary, as recommended by the six-expert panel. This file is a working list only. It is not a completed review, not a certification, and not a change to any live term.

## What to review

For each term, check the Spanish **headword** (and aliases), **definition**, **details**, **example** (setting and text), and **compliance notes** against the English entry and against ordinary borrower Spanish — not word-for-word calque, and not a change to the cited rule. Do not invent regulatory figures. Keep the “Cifras de ejemplo” disclaimer. Mark the row Pass, Revise, or Question, and write a short note.

## How the 50 were chosen

- 14 canonical, borrower-facing TRID, closing-cost, and disclosure terms, including the homepage “Most looked-up by borrowers” topics that have a dedicated term page.
- All 36 current `borrower-faqs` entries (every `faq-*` slug).
- Spot-check samples already done in the sprint (seeds 20261125 and 20261126) are noted below so a reviewer can still confirm them, but need not start from zero. Terms outside this 50 remain in the glossary and can be reviewed in a later pass.

Live paths use the slug as `/es/terms/{slug}`.
